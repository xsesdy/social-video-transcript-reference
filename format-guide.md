# TXT and SRT output formats

## TXT
TXT is plain text without timing information. Choose it when the goal is to read, quote, search, paste into notes, or reuse the words as a script.

## SRT
SRT is a subtitle format. It keeps each transcript line with start and end timecodes so the text can be aligned with the video in a subtitle-compatible editor or player.

## Choosing a format

| Need | Format |
| --- | --- |
| Read or copy the words | TXT |
| Search or quote spoken content | TXT |
| Add timed subtitles to a video | SRT |
| Preserve when each line was spoken | SRT |
